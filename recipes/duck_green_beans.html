Duck Breast with Fig Gastrique & Garlicky Green Beans

Two dishes that plate together. In each card, read left to right: every box to the right of the ingredients is a step, and it spans the ingredients it combines.

Duck Breast with Fig Gastrique

Serves 2About 30 min

2 duck breasts (about 200 g / 7 oz each), skin on, patted dry score skin season skin down in cold pan; medium heat; render 8–10 min flip; 4–6 min to 135°F (57°C) rest, tented, 5–10 min slice; spoon sauce over
Kosher salt & black pepper
1 small shallot, minced sauté in 1 Tbs duck fat, 1–2 min deglaze; reduce to syrup, 3–4 min simmer until it coats a spoon, 5–7 min warm about 1 min off heat; swirl in
¼ cup (60 mL) balsamic vinegar
2 Tbs (40 g) honey
½ cup (120 mL) chicken or duck stock
1 sprig fresh thyme
2 Tbs (30 mL) port or dry red wine (optional)
6 fresh figs, quartered (or 8–10 dried, soaked 10 min)
1 Tbs (15 g) cold unsalted butter, cubed

The sauce is made in the duck pan while the duck rests.

Method

  1. Score the duck skin in a tight crosshatch, cutting through the fat but not into the meat. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Lay the breasts skin side down in a cold, dry skillet, then turn the heat to medium. Starting cold renders the fat slowly, so the skin crisps instead of seizing.
  3. Render for 8–10 minutes, until the skin is deep gold and crisp.
  4. Flip and cook 4–6 minutes more, until the thickest part reads 135°F (57°C) for medium-rare.
  5. Move the duck to a board, tent it with foil, and let it rest 5–10 minutes. Make the sauce while it rests.
  6. Pour off all but about 1 tablespoon of fat from the pan and save the rest. Sauté the shallot over medium heat for 1–2 minutes, until soft.
  7. Add the balsamic and honey and scrape up the browned bits. Reduce until syrupy, 3–4 minutes.
  8. Add the stock, thyme, and port. Simmer 5–7 minutes, until the sauce coats a spoon, then remove the thyme sprig.
  9. Stir in the figs and cook just until they soften, about 1 minute.
  10. Take the pan off the heat and swirl in the cold butter until the sauce is glossy.
  11. Slice the duck on the bias, fan it out, and spoon the sauce over it.

Garlicky Green Beans

Serves 3–4 as a sideAbout 10 min

Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil and set a bowl of ice water next to it
1 lb (450 g) green beans, trimmed blanch 3–4 min shock in ice water; drain; pat dry toss; sauté 2–3 min season & serve
2 Tbs (30 mL) olive oil heat oil over medium; cook garlic & pepper flakes 30–60 sec, until fragrant but not browned
3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
¼–½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
Kosher salt, to taste
Squeeze of lemon juice (optional)
2 Tbs toasted sliced almonds or pine nuts (optional)

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil and set a bowl of ice water next to it.
  2. Blanch the beans for 3–4 minutes, until bright green and just tender.
  3. Move the beans to the ice water to stop the cooking. Drain and pat them dry so they sear in the pan instead of steaming.
  4. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic, plus the pepper flakes if using, and cook 30–60 seconds, until fragrant but not browned.
  5. Add the beans and toss to coat. Sauté 2–3 minutes, until hot with a little char.
  6. Season with salt, then add the lemon and nuts if using. Serve.

Serving together

Serve the beans under or beside the sliced duck and spoon the sauce over both. The garlic and the fig sauce don't compete, since one is savory and sharp and the other is sweet.
